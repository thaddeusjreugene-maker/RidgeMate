Everything you do between the truck and the front door.

One tap logs the knock. Everything else hangs off that pin — the homeowner, the roof, the claim, the photos, the follow-up, the commission.

Knock map

Drop a pin on the satellite map at every door. Color-coded by status, so you can see a whole street at a glance — not home, come back, inspected, sold.

Leads & follow-ups

Every door becomes a lead. Set a follow-up in one tap and get a reminder at 8 AM, so nothing slips through the cracks.

Inspection photos

PDF inspection reports

Questions

Straight answers.

Do I need to download it from the App Store?

No. Open RidgeMate in Safari (iPhone and iPad) or Chrome (Android and computers), then use “Add to Home Screen.” It opens full-screen like any other app and gets its own icon.

Does it work on Android?

Yes. RidgeMate works on Android phones and tablets in Chrome, as well as iPhone, iPad, Mac and Windows.

How does the free trial work?

Create an account and use everything for 14 days. No card is needed to start. If you keep going, you'll pay month to month — no contract.

Can my reps see each other's leads?

Reps see their own leads and the Open Leads board. On the team map they can see that a teammate already knocked a door, but not the homeowner's name or phone. Owners, managers and office staff see everything.

Can I bring my leads from another app?

Yes. Export from your old app or CRM as a spreadsheet (CSV), and RidgeMate's importer matches the columns, puts the addresses on the map and lets you hand them to a rep or post them to the board. You can import your roster the same way.

What happens when a rep quits?

Remove them from the company. Their open leads and pins go back on the Open Leads board — photos, notes and history included — and their sold jobs stay with the company.

Where does the storm data come from?

Hail and wind reports come from the National Weather Service's local storm reports, and alerts come from weather.gov. They're official reports from spotters and the public, not a guarantee that a given roof was hit — always inspect.

Who owns the data?

You do. You can export it any time, and an owner can delete the whole company account from Settings. See our privacy policy.
